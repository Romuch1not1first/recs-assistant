Privacy policy

In effect from 9 September 2026

This document describes what data the AutoSwipe AI Chrome extension and the autoswipeai.com website collect, why it is needed, who it is shared with, and how you can delete it.

In short. We store your email, subscription status and — for subscribers — a summary of your Tinder conversations: who wrote last and when. When you press a button asking for a suggested reply, the open conversation is sent to a text-model provider to write it, and is not stored afterwards; if you have linked that conversation to the same person's chat in another app, the last few messages of that chat travel with it. The conversations themselves are kept only in your browser, so that the assistant does not have to re-read them every time — and the panel menu erases them whenever you want. A voice message you ask to transcribe is sent to a speech-to-text provider and not kept. Of a match that has disappeared we also keep its last two messages, so the dashboard can say what ended it. Filter settings and swipe counters stay in your browser. Profile photos are sent to an external model only when you explicitly enable the AI photo filter and are not stored by us. Your Tinder password is never read.

What we do not collect

Nothing is sent while you simply read or write.
  • Your Tinder login or password — the extension operates inside the session you already opened.
  • Payment card details or wallet addresses — payments are handled entirely by the payment provider.
  • Browsing history outside Tinder, contacts, or any other personal data not listed above.

3. Profile photos (optional AI filter)

When the user explicitly enables the AI looks / body-type filter, the extension sends photos from the currently open Tinder profile card to a third-party image-processing service so that a score can be obtained and used for the swipe decision.

  • Only photos of other users’ profiles shown to you in the Tinder feed are sent — never your own photos.
  • Images are downscaled before transmission and sent over HTTPS.
  • We do not store the images or the scores. The model’s response is used only to decide the current swipe and remains only in the temporary panel log in your browser until the page is reloaded.
  • The AI provider processes the images according to its own privacy policy. The provider API key never leaves our server.

If you do not want any photos to be sent, simply leave the AI photo filters disabled. All other filters (age, height, distance, keywords, verified badge, number of photos) work entirely locally and send nothing.

5. Limited Use commitment

The use of information received from users and from Google APIs (if any) adheres to the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Data is used only to provide and improve the disclosed single purpose of the extension and is not transferred for unrelated purposes.

6. How long we keep data

  • Account data — for as long as the account exists.
  • Payment records — for as long as required by payment-system rules and accounting obligations.
  • Settings and counters in the browser — until you remove the extension or clear the data.
  • Conversation summary — for as long as the account exists; deleting the account removes it. Of a disappeared match only the last two messages are kept: the rest of the tail is discarded as soon as the reason for the unmatch has been worked out.
