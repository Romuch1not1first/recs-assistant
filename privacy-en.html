Privacy policy

In effect from 15 August 2026

This document describes what data the AutoSwipe AI extension and the autoswipeai.com site collect, what it is needed for, who it is shared with and how to delete it.

This document was originally written in Russian. This translation is provided for convenience; in case of any discrepancy the Russian version prevails.

In short. We store only your email and the state of your subscription. Filter settings stay in your browser. Profile photos from the Tinder feed are sent to an external model for scoring and are not kept by us. Tinder chats are never read.

1. Who processes the data

The controller is Roman Tkachenko, Ukraine, trading as AutoSwipe AI. For any question about your data write to support@autoswipeai.com; a postal address is available on request.

What we do not collect

  • Tinder chats — the extension does not read them.
  • Your Tinder login and password — the extension works inside the session you already opened.
  • Card details and your wallet addresses — payment happens on the payment provider's side and never reaches us.
  • Browsing history, geolocation, contacts.

3. Profile photos

To filter profiles by looks and body type, the extension sends photos from the open Tinder card to an external image-processing service. This is a deliberate trade-off: a photo cannot be scored without being sent.

  • What is sent are photos of other people's profiles that Tinder showed you in the feed — not yours.
  • Images are downscaled before sending and travel over a secure connection.
  • We store neither the images nor the scores: the model's answer is used to make the decision and stays only in the panel log in your browser until you reload the page.
  • The processing is done by a third-party model provider; on their side the data is kept according to their own policy.

If you would rather not send photos, turn off the looks and body filters in the panel. The other conditions (age, height, distance, stop-words) are evaluated without sending anything.

We do not sell data and do not share it for advertising.

5. How long we keep it

  • Account data — for as long as the account exists.
  • Payment records — for as long as payment-system rules and accounting require.
  • Settings in the browser — until you delete them along with the extension.

6. Your rights

Write to support@autoswipeai.com to:

  • get a copy of the data we hold;
  • correct data that is wrong;
  • delete your account with all its data;
  • withdraw consent to processing — which stops the service working.

We answer within 30 days.

7. Children

The service is not intended for anyone under 18, as it works with a dating app that has the same restriction.

8. Changes

For material changes we will update the date at the top of this page and notify you at the email address on your account.
